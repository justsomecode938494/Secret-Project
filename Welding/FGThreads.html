High Temp Sewing Threads and Sleevings

A comprehensive range of high-performance sewing threads for insulation, protection, and industrial applications.

Applications

Kevlar threads are ultra-strong, heat-resistant fibers renowned for their exceptional tensile strength and durability. They maintain stability under high temperatures and resist abrasion, making them ideal for stitching, reinforcing, or binding high-performance fabrics such as welding blankets, protective clothing, and insulation materials. Kevlar threads ensure long-lasting performance in demanding industrial environments where strength and thermal resistance are critical.

PTFE Sewing Threads

PTFE-Thread1500 is manufactured from 100% Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE), giving it exceptional lubricity and durability. This composition enables smoother hook pickup, reduced elongation, and higher strength with fewer knots or thread breaks. The PTFE sewing thread is tested to industry standards and offers strong resistance to UV exposure, water, chemicals, mildew, and rot.

Silica Coated Sewing Threads

Silthread12 silica sewing thread offers exceptional tensile strength, ensuring reliable sewing performance. It is highly heat-resistant, with a continuous operating temperature of up to 1800°F (982°C) and the ability to withstand short-term exposure to temperatures as high as 2552°F (1400°C).
